baconmilkshake

3D printing tools and reference data by Robert Samples.

  • MeltCalc

    Compares 3D printer hotends by melt zone geometry: sustainable volumetric flow rate, residence time, melt energy, heater power and cost per mm³/s. Covers 90+ hotends and 36 filament materials from PLA to PEEK.

    meltcalc.baconmilkshake.com

  • INDX-CAD

    Reverse engineered CAD of the Prusa INDX toolhead, including internal geometry and moving assemblies. Live 3D viewer plus STEP and Fusion 360 (.f3z) downloads under CC BY-SA 4.0.

    indx-cad.baconmilkshake.com

  • Toolchanger Data

    A reference for 3D printer toolchanger topologies — conventional toolchangers, filament path changers and hotend changers — with diagrams, tradeoffs, and examples from commercial and open source machines.

    toolchangers.baconmilkshake.com
